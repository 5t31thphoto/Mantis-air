Mantis Airflight for the M5Stack Core2

Tilt to bank, tip to pitch, feel the sky change from noon to night. A living world of fields, coast and stars — flown entirely on the device.

Flash to Core2

Chrome or Edge, USB cable. Same web installer used across the Mantis lineup. CI writes the merged image after every build.

Web flasher needs Chrome / Edge on desktop with WebSerial.

or

Download merged .bin

Manual flash: esptool.py --chip esp32 write_flash 0x0 firmware-merged.bin

Day, dusk and night over fields, water and open sky. Instruments and horizon stay readable on the small display.
